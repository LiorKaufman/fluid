# Fluid: your app, your data, your way

A 65-second explainer for people who do not write software.

The list, board, focus, and tools images are real reference-app captures. The assistant conversations and connection diagram are illustrative. They explain supported workflows rather than depicting a live model response. Voiceover is generated locally with macOS Samantha.

## The idea (0.0s)

Ever liked an app, but wished it looked different? Or worked more like you do?

## Same data (5.7s)

Fluid lets apps built for it keep their data and useful features, while you choose the experience.

## Your views (12.8s)

Take your tasks. View them as a list, a board, or one thing at a time.

## Your flow (19.0s)

Change the view, and your work stays right there. No starting over. No copying everything into a new app.

## Ask an agent (26.8s)

You can also ask your AI assistant: make this simpler, use bigger text, or show only what matters to me.

## Review (35.2s)

The assistant can build a different interface using the same app. You review it and choose its access.

## Data only (42.1s)

Just want the information? A connected assistant can work with the data directly, without using the app's screens.

## Your control (50.3s)

You decide what it can read or change. See the available tools, and turn access off whenever you want.

## Your way (57.9s)

Fluid is the shared set of rules that makes this possible. Your app. Your data. Your way.

## Rebuild

On macOS, install Pillow, NumPy, and imageio-ffmpeg in a virtual environment, then run `python scripts/make-explainer.py` from this project. Assets come from `docs/`; intermediate audio is cached in `.fluid/video-build/`.
